## Why

The prefilled contract can only be downloaded after applying. Applicants submit first, then download, sign, scan and upload from the agreement page: two visits for one application. Everything the contract needs is known while the application form is being filled in: the organization's data, the logged-in admin as representative, and the values typed into the form.

## What Changes

- The application page of a kind with a contract template offers a second button, "Download prefilled contract". It checks the entered values like a submission and returns the contract filled with them, without saving anything or sending emails.
- Invalid input shows the form's errors instead of a document.
- The download keeps the entered values in the form, so the signed contract can be uploaded in the same form before submitting.
- The page notes that the contract must be downloaded again when values change after downloading.

### Non-goals

- Comparing the uploaded signed contract with the submitted values.
- A preview of the SEPA mandate; it needs the mandate reference, which exists only after the agreement.
